How should I store coconut oil?

Keep the container closed and follow the storage directions printed on its label. Coconut oil can change texture with temperature.

Can I use coconut oil on my skin?

Check the product label and test a small area first. Personal-care products can affect people differently; stop use if irritation occurs.

Where do I find ingredients and product details?

Review the label on each item for its ingredients, directions, and any product-specific information.
